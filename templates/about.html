About this project

Clear, accessible, and reliable health information: surveillance numbers where they exist, and plain-language disease guidance for everyone.

Our mission

Provide clear, accessible, and reliable health information for the public — verified dataset numbers presented simply, plus educational disease pages based on WHO, MoHFW, and NCDC public guidance.

What we track

State and district surveillance from the project dataset ({{ site.source_label }} — {{ site.source_detail }}), updated {{ site.surveillance_updated or 'date not available' }}. The disease library ({{ library_count }} conditions, updated {{ site.library_updated or 'date not available' }}) is educational and carries no case counts.

Features

  • Dashboard overview with KPIs calculated from the live dataset
  • Searchable, sortable states table with district drill-down
  • Disease library with category filters, A–Z index, and detail pages
  • Analytics charts with disease and top-N filters plus ranked tables
  • Responsive, keyboard-accessible design with honest empty states
