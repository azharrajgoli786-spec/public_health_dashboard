About This Project

Our Mission

This project is designed to provide clear, accessible, and reliable health information for the public. We collect disease data from different regions, verify it for accuracy, and present it in an easy-to-understand format.

What We Track

Our dashboard monitors disease outbreaks across Indian states and districts, tracking Dengue, Malaria, Typhoid, Cholera, and Chickenpox with transparent case reporting.

Features

  • Disease tracking across states
  • District-level case statistics
  • Search with autocomplete and partial matching
  • Ranked charts plus accessible data tables
  • Mobile-responsive, keyboard-accessible design
  • Transparent, open-source health data

Why It Matters

Transparent health data empowers communities, supports early outbreak detection, and helps medical teams respond faster — contributing to a more aware, informed, and healthier society.
